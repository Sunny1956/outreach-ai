Company

Every channel a lead lives on, reachable from one login.

Oureach.ai is a unified outreach platform: connect your email and your Instagram, Facebook, Twitter and LinkedIn once, upload a list of leads, and run outreach campaigns across all five channels while a single dashboard tracks what got sent, what landed, and what still needs a follow-up.

The problem

Sales and support teams juggle five separate inboxes and apps to reach the same lead, copy-pasting the same message and losing track of who's already been contacted where.

The approach

One login, one lead list, one message template. Oureach.ai fans a campaign out across every connected channel and logs every attempt in a single activity feed.

Who it's for

Built as a college capstone around a real workflow: small sales teams and solo founders who can't justify five separate outreach subscriptions.

Parv Patel

Founder & CEO, Oureach.ai

Parv Patel is the founder and CEO of Oureach.ai, a computer engineering student at Ganpat University who built the platform out of a problem he watched up close from both sides of the sales floor and the support desk: reaching the same lead across five different apps, with no single place to see whether any of it actually landed.

As a Sales Executive at Smarty Agency, Parv spent close to three years developing and maintaining client relationships through direct outreach and follow-up, running market research to size up prospects, and pitching tailored proposals — work that meant living inside inboxes, DMs and social profiles every day and feeling the friction of doing it all by hand. Alongside that, as a Technical Support Engineer at Gurukrupa Industries, he's spent his time diagnosing hardware issues for a broad client base, feeding user feedback back to engineering teams, and training new staff on troubleshooting and service protocols — building the habit of turning a repeated manual process into a documented, teachable system.

Oureach.ai is that instinct applied to outreach itself: a single dashboard where connecting an email account and a set of social handles is a one-time setup, a lead list becomes a campaign in minutes, and every send, success and failure is logged automatically instead of tracked across five open tabs.

Parv is currently completing his Bachelor of Technology in Computer Engineering at Ganpat University, Kherva (Mehsana), expected 2027, and is based in Palanpur, India.
