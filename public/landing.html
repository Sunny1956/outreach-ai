What you get

Everything for multi-channel outreach

Built for founders, creators, and marketers who don't want five separate tools.

Real email delivery

Connect any Gmail or SMTP account with an app password. Oureach.ai sends real emails directly from your account so replies land in your inbox, not a shared platform.

SMTP · Works with Gmail, Outlook, custom

Social platform reach

Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, and LinkedIn — all in one campaign. Map leads to their social handles and send personalised DMs or posts from a single compose window.

Demo mode ships out of the box

Smart lead management

Import hundreds of leads from a CSV with one click, or add them manually. Search, filter, and browse leads instantly. Include only the leads you want in each campaign.

CSV import · Manual add · Live search

Live campaign analytics

See every send, failure, and result the moment it happens. The activity feed updates in real time — per lead, per channel, per campaign — so you always know what's landing.

Per-lead result tracking

In-app notifications

A notification bell keeps you updated without interrupting your flow. Get alerts for successful sends, failures, newly imported leads, and connected accounts — all in a tidy dropdown.

Real-time · Unread count badge

OTP email verification

Every new account is verified with a 6-digit code sent to your inbox. Keeps the platform secure, spam-free, and trusted. Works in demo mode too — the code is shown on screen.

6-digit OTP · 10-min expiry · Rate limited
